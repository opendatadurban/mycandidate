Empowering voters with information they can trust

MyCandidate is a free, open source platform that gives voters easy-to-understand electoral information ahead of an election, so they can make informed choices at the ballot box.

View a live deployment Read the technical docs

About the project

MyCandidate is built and maintained by Open Cities Lab together with the open source community, including this repository's original maintainer, OpenDataDurban. The platform collects publicly available candidate information — profiles, manifestos and demographic data — and presents it in a simple web tool that anyone can use to research who is standing in their area before they vote.

The project has been deployed to support elections in several African countries, including South Africa, Kenya, Nigeria, Sierra Leone and Zimbabwe, and is designed to be re-deployed for new elections and new countries.

What it does

Candidate profiles

Photos, biographies and manifestos for candidates, organised by ward, constituency or region.

Find your candidates

Voters can search by dropdown, type in an address, or use "locate me" geolocation to find the candidates standing in their area.

Voter education

Plain-language content that explains the voting process alongside the candidate data.

Flexible deployment

From a simple no-code Webflow site to a full web application, MyCandidate scales to match an organisation's capacity and timeline.
